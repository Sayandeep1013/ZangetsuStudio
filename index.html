HOW EACH UNIVERSE IS MADE
  1. 01

    INK

    Original manga art, run through one ink pass so every page reads black, paper and one accent colour.

  2. 02

    STORY

    The arc is broken into chapters. Every scroll scene is a beat, and the prologue always pays off later.

  3. 03

    SCROLL

    GSAP and Lenis turn the scroll into the camera: pins, cuts, impact frames and wipes.
